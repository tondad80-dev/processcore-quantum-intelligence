Evidence Intelligence Layer

Unlike traditional static scrapers, every record in ProcessCore Golden DB includes verified provenance chains:

  • ✓ Source URL: Direct primary trace back to official web or registry.
  • ✓ Validation Timestamp: Exact temporal record of discovery and check.
  • ✓ Technology Signals: Extracted stack and vector markers.
  • ✓ Confidence Score: Quantified estimate of entity validity based on evidence signals.
