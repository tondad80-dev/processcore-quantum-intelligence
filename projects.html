OUTDOORS / PERSONAL SOFTWARE

HoubarSky

Closer to the forest.

Public alpha

Evidence date
2026-11-01

Open HoubarSky ↗

Diary and privacy ↗

The question
Keep outing plans, photographs and field notes together without making a private diary public.
The approach
A browser-based application with local diary storage, trip planning, photographs and backup export.
Privacy and visits
The diary stays on your device. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate visits and website performance without analytics cookies. Diary photographs, coordinates and notes are not sent as part of these measurements.
Available to inspect
Public alpha 0.7.23 offers a local diary, trip planning, three tool levels and an optional game theme. The introduction has six languages; translations of the whole application remain partial.
Evidence boundary
Suggested locations and routes require checking. The experimental species tools do not establish edibility.
Stated direction
Learn from one completed outing by another user and use that feedback to improve the first-use experience.

Technology in the rhythm of life.

Design & care records

Evidence date
2026-11-01

Explore Sirona ↗
The question
Understand the needs of a living environment before entrusting its care to automation.
The approach
Aqua and Tera lead from a focal species through environment design to personal care. Manual measurements, care and photographs remain in local records.
Available to inspect
Species-based designs for Tanganyika and a leopard gecko, personal care records, a photo diary and backup. The educational VPD model and synthetic Aqua recording remain separate demonstrations.

Stated direction
The stated direction is to connect observations and measurements to checked control of a physical environment.

ENGINEERING / AI SOFTWARE

ProcessCore

Evidence before interpretation.

Governed software factory / in development

Evidence date
2026-11-01

Inspect the engineering ↗

Engineering details are in English

The question
Distinguish a generated answer or a claimed success from what was actually checked and accepted.
The approach
The engineering project separates specifications, generation, recorded checks, acceptance and permission to deploy.
Available to inspect
ProcessCore 1.0.6 and Factory 0.1.6-rc.1 are accepted and installed on Raspberry Pi, with Gemini, an approved-task queue and bounded repository-task support. An earlier accepted tool was used internally on real manifests. The first repository result passed 20 prescribed test methods, but subsequent review found an edge-case parsing error.
Evidence boundary
Runtime adoption is separate from accepting generated software. That first repository result is not accepted or packaged; passing the prescribed tests did not resolve the review finding. General software correctness, adversarial independence of repository checks and time savings are not established.
Stated direction
Resolve the review finding in a separately authorized repair task, then review the result before any acceptance. The pilot comparing total human effort and cost has not started.

The approach
A separate data-acquisition and catalog workflow, with its own processing and acceptance decisions.
