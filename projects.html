OUTDOORS / PERSONAL SOFTWARE

HoubarSky

Closer to the forest.

Public alpha

Reviewed
18 Nov 2026

Open HoubarSky (opens in a new window)

Diary and privacy (opens in a new window)

The question
Keep outing plans, photographs and field notes together without making a private diary public.
The approach
A browser-based application with local diary storage, trip planning, photographs and backup export.
Privacy and visits
The diary stays on your device. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate visits and website performance without analytics cookies. Diary photographs, coordinates and notes are not sent as part of these measurements.
Available to inspect
Public alpha 0.7.40 offers a local diary, trip planning with public-transport preparation, password-protected backups and three tool levels. The introduction offers six languages. Translation coverage varies across the rest of the app. Check deployed version (opens in a new window)
Forest School
Forest School lessons, questions and answers are available in Czech, English, Slovak, Polish, German and Ukrainian. Lesson text and images can be prepared for offline use. Optional narration uses an available device voice for the selected language. The bird quiz uses eight field recordings, and 0.7.40 improves the bird cards on mobile. Independent expert review and family testing are still unverified.
Public sources and personal observations
The planner links to public reports and a separately labelled growth forecast. The local diary can also record a search with no finds. External sites open only after a click; their data is not incorporated into recommendations. The new version alone does not establish identification accuracy or an outing outcome.
Evidence boundary
The diary and forms work offline after preparing the application. Maps, weather, server routing and optional AI analysis need a connection. A species suggestion does not establish edibility; routes need checking.
Stated direction
Learn from one completed outing by another user and use that feedback to improve the first-use experience.

LIVING SYSTEMS / TERA + AQUA

Sirona

Technology in the rhythm of life.

Design & care records

Reviewed
18 Nov 2026

Explore Sirona (opens in a new window)
The question
Understand the needs of a living environment before entrusting its care to automation.
The approach
Aqua and Tera lead from a focal species through environment design to personal care. My habitats keeps designs and photographs in the browser. The separate private care diary stores manual readings, care notes and photographs on the server after sign-in.
Available to inspect
A species-based design workshop, equipment list, own-build guidance and a private care/photo diary (opens in a new window) requiring sign-in. The public catalogue has 16 equipment models with sources and dated price observations. It leads to non-binding enquiries about equipment, custom assembly, or advice and maintenance.
Diary storage
Care records and diary photographs are stored on the server with owner checks. Local My habitats records and the server diary are not automatically synchronized. The diary alone does not establish an operational pilot or verified sensor integration.
Local backup · 15 November
Version 53, published on 15 November, added an encrypted backup of local habitats (opens in a new window), their history, original photographs and references. The backup and a separate recovery key are downloaded to the device; keep the key separately. The private server diary and enquiries are excluded. Restoration requires an empty local destination; transfer between actual devices still needs verification.
Public version 63 · 16 November
After preparing an encrypted backup, the user can explicitly choose to share it through a system application on a supported device. The user selects the destination; the recovery key is not attached and must be kept separately. File download remains available without sharing support. The actual Android dialog and transfer between different devices or browsers still need verification.
Public version 80 · 18 November
Visitors can open three species templates. In the private area, an owner can simulate a lamp outage or a stale or missing probe reading. These scenarios run in a model. Physical sensors and control of a living habitat require separate verification.
Model demonstrations
The public scenario cinema and control comparison let visitors inspect modelled Aqua/Tera development and share a link to a particular moment. These are software simulations; they do not establish measurements from an actual habitat, biological acceptance or physical control operation.
Evidence boundary
Equipment suitability, service scope, price and availability need confirmation. Catalogue price observations are not confirmed Sirona offers or stock. Direct sales and smart control are in development. AI images are concepts; physical sensors, automatic biological assessment and autonomous operation are not verified features.
Stated direction
Connect design, equipment, construction and care through shared records. Connection to a physical environment requires its own verification.

AGRICULTURE / GRANTS AND DIRECT PAYMENTS

Agricultural Grant Advisory

Check documents and deadlines.

Pilot using fictional cases

Reviewed
18 Nov 2026

Advisory website (CZ) (opens in a new window)

Discuss a precheck

Fictional pre-check walkthrough (Czech) →

The question
How can applicants identify which documents and deadlines need attention before submission or an inspection?
The approach
The documented workflow has nine phases from the initial investment plan to post-project obligations. A specific phase can be discussed: the advisor reviews relevant records and provides findings and next steps.
Advisory services
Kosířská A + H s.r.o. offers prechecks for investment grants, LEADER projects and the Czech Single Application, including direct payments and LPIS land records. To compare LPIS records, the applicant supplies a separate XML export for the relevant date.
Pilot scope
The trial tool uses fictional cases. Initial coverage includes intervention 33.73 and agricultural projects under 52.77 LEADER in the Olomouc and Zlín regions. A controlled assisted simulation on 13 November identified all three predefined discrepancies. This was a known test set, not an independent accuracy test. Complete human effort and paid acceptance remain unestablished.
Document-photo intake
The deployed pilot lets users prepare document photographs, preview them and submit the original files to a case. Preparation alone does not upload them. Attachment intake and the preview correction have technical operational verification; this does not establish assessment of their contents.
Company identity · 15 November
The deployed pilot identifies Kosířská A + H s.r.o. as the provider, with consistent company identity across attachments and client/advisor outputs. A signed-in live Justice-register call remains unverified; deployed identity alone does not establish a complete applicant check or client acceptance.
Technical PDF check
An advisor can check each page of an uploaded PDF for a readable text layer. The result shows where text can be extracted. OCR, substantive document review and acceptance of the complete client workflow require separate verification.
Prepared attachment assessment
AI attachment assessment and a draft request for advisor review and approval are prepared implementations tested with a substitute provider. Live AI reading, recognition of illegibility or factual errors and the complete client workflow are not verified operational features.
Next step
Measure the advisor’s actual work on the prepared sample, including preparation, verification, corrections and handover. Preserve the completed assisted simulation as a separate result.
First conversation
Describe the grant or payment, the stage of the application and the region. Arrange secure document sharing with the advisor after the initial enquiry.
