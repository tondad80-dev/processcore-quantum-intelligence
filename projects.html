OUTDOORS / PERSONAL SOFTWARE

HoubarSky

Closer to the forest.

Public alpha

Evidence date
2026-11-08

Open HoubarSky ↗

Diary and privacy ↗

The question
Keep outing plans, photographs and field notes together without making a private diary public.
The approach
A browser-based application with local diary storage, trip planning, photographs and backup export.
Privacy and visits
The diary stays on your device. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate visits and website performance without analytics cookies. Diary photographs, coordinates and notes are not sent as part of these measurements.
Available to inspect
Public alpha 0.7.25 has a local diary, trip planning including public-transport preparation, password-protected backup export and three tool levels. The introduction offers six languages; translations of the whole application remain partial.
Evidence boundary
The diary and forms work offline after preparing the application. Maps, weather, server routing and optional AI analysis need a connection. A species suggestion does not establish edibility; routes need checking.
Stated direction
Learn from one completed outing by another user and use that feedback to improve the first-use experience.

LIVING SYSTEMS / TERA + AQUA

Sirona

Technology in the rhythm of life.

Design & care records

Evidence date
2026-11-13

Explore Sirona ↗
The question
Understand the needs of a living environment before entrusting its care to automation.
The approach
Aqua and Tera lead from a focal species through environment design to personal care. Manual measurements, care notes and photographs can be stored in a private care diary after sign-in.
Available to inspect
A species-based design workshop, equipment list, own-build guidance and a private care/photo diary requiring sign-in. The public catalogue has 16 equipment models with sources and dated price observations. It leads to non-binding enquiries about equipment, custom assembly, or advice and maintenance.
Diary storage
Care records and diary photographs are stored on the server with owner checks. The earlier local diary is not automatically synchronized. The diary alone does not establish an operational pilot or verified sensor integration.
Evidence boundary
Equipment suitability, service scope, price and availability need confirmation. Catalogue price observations are not confirmed Sirona offers or stock. Direct sales and smart control are in development. AI images are concepts; physical sensors, automatic biological assessment and autonomous operation are not verified features.
Stated direction
Connect design, equipment, construction and care through shared records. Connection to a physical environment requires its own verification.

The question
Distinguish a generated answer or a claimed success from what was actually checked and accepted.
The approach
The engineering project separates specifications, generation, recorded checks, acceptance and permission to deploy.
Available to inspect
ProcessCore 1.0.9 on Raspberry Pi; limited pytest support was accepted into the main Factory runtime on 13 November. A ZIP-inspection correction passed 23 tests, acceptance and packaging on 5 November, then inspected a real package in a dedicated Lab copy. The 7 November ZIP command records 29 completed tests and actual Lab use. Its separate delivery-check utility was upgraded to 0.1.1 on 9 November (CET). Recorded checks accepted the matching package and rejected a different expected job or archive hash; the installed files still match their recorded hashes. On 8 November, Factory-generated duplicate-title grouping was merged in an external project: agentswarm-data-tasks PR #6 ↗. On 9 November, trustgate PR #18 ↗ was merged: a check for unpinned MCP launcher packages, prepared through Factory and corrected during subsequent Codex review.
Latest accepted contribution
On 12 November the maintainer merged trustgate PR #19 ↗ for Cursor and VS Code Insiders profiles. AI review was followed by a Codex Windows-path correction and regression test; nine CI checks passed on the submitted commit.
Limited pytest support · 13 November
Adoption into the main runtime verified installation and preservation of historical task records. Adoption itself did not run a new production pytest or model task. Candidate and tests still share an interpreter; independent review and general support for arbitrary projects are unestablished. Read the acceptance scope →
Installed internal tools
The external-delivery receipt summary remains a separate 0.1.0 command. GitHub Evidence was upgraded to 0.1.2 on 13 November: it captures and replays GitHub and CI evidence while distinguishing the CI result from its binding to a particular pull request. Successful capture alone does not mean CI passed. Both commands have their own acceptance, separate from the main Factory runtime.
Lab status integration · 12 November
A separate Lab command links a Factory task and its original result to a dated GitHub observation. It requested a refresh for an expired snapshot, then matched a fresh capture to the task and recorded the upstream merge. Eighteen native checks passed. This integration is not deployed into the main Factory runtime and does not track the current commit continuously. Read the result and limits →
Lab experiment
An SSD–QCore–Factory task context was used in one Gemini review, followed by host verification of the response. The model supplied no supported finding. The merged probe source and this experiment do not establish automatic awareness of the whole portfolio or production integration. The later storage migration has separate operational evidence in the QCore note below.
Evidence boundary
Lab use, internal utility installation and external contribution acceptance each have their own scope. The PR #6 acceptance comment identifies itself as a project AI agent; human review is unverified, and synthetic benchmark scores do not establish general reliability. Trustgate’s submitted revision includes a post-generation correction; merging does not prove independent human review or deployment by downstream users. For repository tasks, candidate and tests share a process; those tests do not establish independent protection against adversarial code. Earlier failed attempts remain preserved. Total human effort, comparative costs and customer benefit are unmeasured.
Stated direction
Evaluate further bounded uses and measure the work required for review and handover. Acceptance and deployment each need their own decision.

The question
Prepare entity profiles whose source material and accepted content can be inspected.
The approach
A separate data-acquisition and catalog workflow, with its own processing and acceptance decisions.
Available to inspect
The 0.22 pilot finished on 30 October after more than 24 hours: 20 proposals, 2 accepted and 18 deferred. It missed the predefined minimum of three new internal profiles; acceptance is FAIL. The earlier 0.21 pilot keeps its own result.
Storage and recovery · 12 November
The data directory was moved to SSD. A real Pi restart verified the mount, automatic return of all three services and a later scheduled intake read. Snapshots of two QCore databases were stored outside the Pi and restored on another computer; file hashes, schemas, row counts and SQLite integrity checks matched. This is a one-off restoration of two snapshots, not a whole-Pi backup or recurring backup schedule. Read the storage evidence →
Evidence boundary
These are dated results, not live telemetry. Storage recovery does not validate catalog content or change the pilot’s FAIL result. Internal profile acceptance is separate from a Golden release and does not establish independent accuracy or customer value. Application speed gains remain unmeasured.
Stated direction
Assess newly accepted profile content against the catalog purpose using the current QCore project evidence.

Agricultural Investment Grant Advisory

Check documents and deadlines.

Pilot using fictional cases

Evidence date
2026-11-13

Discuss a precheck ↗
The question
How can applicants identify which documents and deadlines need attention before submission or an inspection?
The approach
The documented workflow has nine phases from the initial investment plan to post-project obligations. A specific phase can be discussed: the advisor reviews relevant records and provides findings and next steps.
Pilot scope
The trial tool uses fictional cases. Initial coverage includes intervention 33.73 and agricultural projects under 52.77 LEADER in the Olomouc and Zlín regions. A controlled assisted simulation on 13 November identified all three predefined discrepancies. This was a known test set, not an independent accuracy test. Complete human effort and paid acceptance remain unestablished.
Next step
Measure the advisor’s actual work on the prepared sample, including preparation, verification, corrections and handover. Preserve the completed assisted simulation as a separate result.
First conversation
Describe the investment type, application stage and region. Do not send the application or private supporting records with an initial enquiry.
