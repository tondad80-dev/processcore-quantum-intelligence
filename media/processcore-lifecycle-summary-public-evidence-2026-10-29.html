What remains open

Keep the result inside its evidence

  • The utility projects a supplied report. It does not verify report provenance or determine whether the reported execution actually occurred.
  • Review was AI-assisted. Independently human-authored validation, a hidden holdout and independent external reproduction are not established by these records.
  • The accepted review records a numeric-range interpretation; arbitrary-precision or unlimited-range JSON input is not guaranteed.
  • A task-specific result on one acceptance host and the separate native Pi checks do not establish arbitrary repository support, general portability or general production readiness.
  • Time savings, total generation cost and customer benefit have not been measured. No cost or efficiency advantage follows from the passing counts.
